Managed WordPress hosting, easy to manage

We help with WordPress setup, SSL, available backups and restores, typical-site migration, PHP settings and caching guidance. Request core updates or platform security help through support.

What you get

  • One-click WordPress, free Let's Encrypt SSL, Daily backups and restore assistance.
  • PHP tuning help, caching guidance and platform malware response.
  • WordPress core updates on request — never a silent plugin or WooCommerce change.
  • Real storage: 7 GB, 20 GB or 30 GB — not unlimited.
  • 1, 3 or 10 WordPress sites, depending on the card.
  • Guided migration for a typical site after you send the old login.
  • Rupee invoices and JazzCash / EasyPaisa / bank / card.

What this is not

  • Not a Kinsta or WP Engine clone. No object-cache platform claim.
  • Not Kinsta-style theme, plugin or WooCommerce management.
  • Not unlimited disk or unlimited sites.
  • Zero downtime is not guaranteed — staged cutover only.
  • The free .com on a 3-year term is on shared hosting, not these WP cards.
What managed means

Platform work we do — and work that stays in your admin

Managed covers

  • One-click WordPress install and free SSL with auto-renewal
  • Daily backups and restore assistance from copies still listed
  • Guided migration for a typical WordPress site
  • PHP version and tuning help, plus caching guidance
  • Platform malware response on the hosting stack
  • WordPress core updates on request — not silent automatic changes

Managed does not cover

  • Theme, plugin or WooCommerce updates (those stay under your admin)
  • A Kinsta or WP Engine object-cache platform
  • A separate staging environment on these WP cards
  • Unlimited disk, unlimited sites, or a contractual uptime SLA

1-click WordPress

Install WordPress from your dashboard in one click — no downloads, no database setup, with your administrator login provided after setup.

Guided migration

We move a typical WordPress site, database and mail when you give us the old host login. We use a staged migration designed to minimize downtime. DNS propagation, application behavior and the old provider can still cause temporary interruption, so zero downtime is not guaranteed.

Free SSL, auto-renewed

A free Let's Encrypt certificate installs in one click and renews itself, so the site is served over HTTPS.

Daily backups

Every plan is backed up daily. Copies listed in your dashboard include website files and the database (and email when mailboxes exist). Restores from listed copies are included; keep an independent copy. Best-effort recovery — no contractual RTO.

Fast SSD, tuned for WP

SSD storage and sensible defaults keep WordPress and its admin quick as your content and traffic grow.

A real team, on your time

Talk to people in Pakistan — live chat and a direct line Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, email on weekends, and tickets answered every day.

Priced in rupees

Pay in PKR by JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer or card. No dollars, no forex, no international card required.

Domain + email in one place

Manage your .pk or .com domain, hosting and business email from one account. Domain and hosting terms can expire on different dates; review each renewal in your dashboard.
